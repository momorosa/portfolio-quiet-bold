# Quiet Bold — Rosa Choi's Portfolio

Senior product lead based in San Francisco. This is the source for **[quietbold.com](https://quietbold.com)** — a portfolio of work across AI product design, real-time 3D, and front-end engineering.

---

## Featured Work

**TomoCare**
A solo-built agentic AI sidekick for proactive pet care, and a working testbed for the governed-AI patterns I want to bring into enterprise work. Two phases shipped: a Phase 0 ingestion pipeline that parses unstructured vet documents into a verified health timeline with auto-generated calendar reminders, and a Phase 1 verification UI where the human approves, edits, or rejects AI-extracted facts before they enter the trusted record.

**Chef Claude**
LLM-powered cooking assistant. Claude Haiku 4.5 + DALL·E 3, end-to-end product build, live on Vercel.

**AI Character to Life**
Multi-model pipeline (image generation → voice synthesis → lip-sync video) for bringing a fictional character to life across three AI systems.

**Wizard Game**
Browser-based spellcasting game in React Three Fiber. State machines, particle VFX, positional audio, and a Zustand-driven real-time game loop.

**The Lost Prince**
Solo-built VR puzzle adventure in Unity and C#, reimagining The Little Prince through non-linear environmental storytelling.

**Portals Effect**
3D portal prototype for spatial scene navigation. Programmatic camera choreography and real-time animated blending.

**Image Transition Effect**
WebGL study comparing noise and displacement shader hover transitions. GPU-level motion design with GLSL.
